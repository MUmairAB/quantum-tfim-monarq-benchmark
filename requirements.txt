# Top-level dependencies; pip resolves the rest. Tested on macOS/CPU.
# numpy pinned to 2.4.2, not the newer 2.5.2, to match the newest version
# available in Compute Canada's constrained wheelhouse (numpy/scipy/mpi4py/
# pandas/h5py/Cython/grpcio are locked there for binary compatibility with
# their optimized builds) — keeps both environments on the same version.

# qiskit 2.5.0, not 2.5.2 — newest version with a Compute Canada wheel.

# circuit simulation on lightning.qubit with adjoint differentiation (fast,

# docstring for why default.qubit isn't used here). pennylane-lightning ships
# as part of a plain `pennylane` install, but pinned explicitly since it's a
# direct dependency now, not just incidental.

pennylane-lightning==0.45.0
